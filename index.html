The Man Behind The Legend

About Virat Kohli

Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, Virat Kohli is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. From the dusty lanes of Delhi to the grandest stadiums in the world, his journey is a testament to unwavering determination, relentless hard work, and an insatiable hunger for success.

Kohli's passion for cricket ignited at the tender age of three when he picked up a bat and began swinging. Trained under coach Rajkumar Sharma at the West Delhi Cricket Academy, young Virat showed extraordinary promise that would soon captivate the entire nation.

His father, Prem Kohli, a criminal lawyer, was his biggest supporter, driving him to practice sessions every day. The tragic loss of his father during a Ranji Trophy match in 2006 became a defining moment — Virat returned to bat the very next day, showcasing the mental fortitude that would define his career.

The Journey

From Streets to Stardom

A chronicle of determination, passion, and unmatched excellence that shaped the King of Cricket

1998

The Beginning

At age 9, young Virat joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy under coach Rajkumar Sharma. His natural talent and aggressive playing style were evident from the very first session.

2002

Delhi Cricket

Selected for Delhi's Under-15 team, Kohli began dominating age-group cricket. His scores consistently placed him among the top performers in the region.

2008

U-19 World Cup Glory

Captain Kohli led India to victory in the ICC U-19 Cricket World Cup in Malaysia. His leadership and batting prowess announced a future superstar to the world.

2008

International Debut

Made his ODI debut against Sri Lanka on August 18, 2008, at just 19 years old. The journey from Delhi's local grounds to the international stage had begun.

2011

World Cup Winner

Part of India's historic 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup winning squad. Kohli's contributions with the bat were instrumental throughout the tournament.

2013

Rise in ODI Cricket

Became the fastest batsman to reach 5,000 ODI runs. His consistency in run-chases earned him the nickname "Chase Master" as he dominated world cricket.

2014

Test Cricket Success

Stunning debut series as Test captain in Australia, scoring 692 runs including 4 centuries. Established himself as the backbone of India's Test batting.

2017

Captaincy Era

Led India to the No. 1 ranking in Test cricket. Under his captaincy, India became a dominant force in world cricket with an aggressive, fearless approach.

2018

Fitness Revolution

Transformed Indian cricket's fitness culture completely. His personal transformation from chubby teenager to world-class athlete inspired an entire generation.

2024

Modern Legend

With 80+ international centuries and 27,000+ runs, Kohli's legacy is cemented as one of the greatest cricketers of all time. The King continues to reign.

Royal Challengers Bengaluru

The Loyalty & Legacy

Since 2008: The only player to represent a single franchise for 17+ years in the IPL.

Captain, Leader, Legend

Virat Kohli's association with RCB is more than just cricket; it's an emotion for millions of fans. From joining as a teenager in the inaugural 2008 season to becoming the highest run-scorer in the tournament's history, his loyalty to the franchise is unmatched.

Under his captaincy, RCB reached the finals in 2016, a season where he shattered all records by scoring an unbelievable 973 runs and 4 centuries. His passion on the field and unyielding commitment to the red and gold jersey have made him synonymous with the team.

The Transformation

Fitness Revolution

How Virat Kohli transformed himself and Indian cricket's fitness culture forever

From Transformation to Revolution

Virat Kohli's fitness journey is arguably the most remarkable transformation in cricket history. From an overweight teenager who thrived on butter chicken and chole bhature, Kohli transformed himself into one of the fittest athletes in world sport.

His strict vegan diet, intense workout routines, and unwavering discipline raised the fitness standards for the entire Indian cricket team. Under his influence, the BCCI introduced mandatory fitness tests — the famous "Yo-Yo Test" — which became a prerequisite for team selection.

Impact & Legacy

Revolutionizing Indian Cricket

Under Kohli's influence, Indian cricket transformed from a predominantly spin-friendly, home-track-bully team to a formidable force that wins overseas series. His aggressive brand of cricket changed the team's DNA forever.

02

Inspiring a Generation

Millions of young cricketers across India look up to Kohli not just for his batting, but for his attitude, fitness, and never-give-up spirit. He proved that talent combined with hard work creates legends.

03

Leadership Legacy

As captain, Kohli's win percentage was among the highest in cricket history. He instilled a culture of excellence, aggression, and accountability that transformed Indian cricket's mentality on the world stage.

04
